# AURA — Charter of Independence and Scope

## 1. Purpose

AURA is an open, neutral technical reference framework dedicated to proof of origin, provenance, and transparency of digital works in the context of AI systems.

Its sole purpose is to provide a **verifiable, auditable, and interoperable evidentiary layer** enabling the expression and verification of:

- origin claims,

- human authorship signals,

- consent signals,

- TDM opt-out declarations,

in alignment with applicable regulatory frameworks, including the European AI Act.

AURA is designed as a **reference layer**, not as an operational or enforcement system.

---

## 2. Principle of Independence

AURA is **structurally independent** from:

- any platform, distributor, or content host,

- any commercial exploitation or licensing scheme,

- any public or private enforcement authority,

- any DRM, watermarking, fingerprinting, monitoring, or content control mechanism.

No actor may claim exclusivity, ownership, or privileged control over AURA.

---

## 3. Non-Execution and Non-Enforcement

AURA does **not**:

- enforce rights,

- block, restrict, or alter content,

- monitor usage or behavior,

- perform surveillance or automated compliance actions.

AURA produces **verifiable signals and proofs only**.  

All interpretation, enforcement, or legal action remains strictly outside the scope of AURA and under the responsibility of third parties.

---

## 4. Platform Neutrality

AURA is:

- platform-independent,

- implementation-agnostic,

- usable across public and private infrastructures.

No platform-specific dependency, integration requirement, or technical coupling shall be introduced into the core specification.

---

## 5. Auditability and Transparency

All core mechanisms defined by AURA must be:

- technically auditable by independent third parties,

- documented in a transparent manner,

- reviewable without reliance on proprietary black-box components.

Security through obscurity is explicitly excluded.

---

## 6. Privacy and Data Minimization

AURA must remain compatible with privacy-by-design and data-minimization principles.

AURA does **not** require personal data to function.

Implementations should prefer:

- cryptographic hashes instead of embedded content,

- issuer or registry identifiers instead of unnecessary personal identifiers,

- pseudonymous, institutional, or role-based identifiers where appropriate,

- offline verification where possible.

AURA must not be presented as a system for profiling, monitoring, behavioural tracking, automated enforcement, or automated legal decision-making.

Any implementation adding personal data remains responsible for its own legal basis, transparency duties, retention policy, data-subject rights, and applicable GDPR/privacy obligations.

---

## 7. Separation from Control Technologies

AURA is **explicitly distinct** from:

- DRM systems,

- content identification or tracking systems,

- automated moderation tools,

- usage monitoring infrastructures.

Any implementation combining AURA with such systems must clearly separate AURA from those components and may not present AURA as responsible for their operation.

---

## 8. Governance Constraint

No evolution of AURA may:

- introduce enforcement logic into the core specification,

- compromise neutrality or independence,

- transform AURA into a control, monitoring, or compliance-execution system.

Any such change constitutes a violation of this Charter.

---

## 9. Relationship to AUTHENTICA

AUTHENTICA is a separate proof and traceability infrastructure that may support experimental or applied implementations related to AURA.

The existence of AUTHENTICA does **not** alter the neutral, non-executive, and independent nature of AURA as a reference framework.

---

## 10. Irreversibility

This Charter defines **non-negotiable structural constraints**.

Any fork, derivative work, or implementation claiming alignment with AURA must either:

- comply with this Charter in full, or

- explicitly state non-compliance.

Silence does not constitute compliance.

---

## 11. Final Statement

AURA exists to make provenance and origin **provable**, not enforceable.

Its value lies precisely in what it refuses to become.
