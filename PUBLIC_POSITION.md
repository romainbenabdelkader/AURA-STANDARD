# AURA Public Position

Status: public scope statement.
Last updated: 2026-06-09.

AURA is a technical standard initiative for signed origin declarations and asset integrity. It produces verifiable artefacts. It does not create, remove, condition, transfer or adjudicate legal rights.

## Creator Rights

AURA does not remove any rights from creators, authors, performers, publishers, producers, labels, institutions or rightsholders.

The absence of an AURA manifest does not mean that a work is unprotected, public domain, free to use, or available for AI training.

AURA does not make technical registration a condition for copyright protection, related-rights protection, contractual protection or any other legal protection.

## Collective Management And Existing Systems

AURA does not replace collective management organizations, CMOs, OGCs, public authorities, registries, regulators, courts, copyright offices, contractual systems, identifiers or industry standards.

AURA is designed to coexist with systems such as ISRC, ISWC, DDEX, C2PA and sector-specific rights-management workflows.

## Platforms And Automated Decisions

AURA does not give platforms, AI providers, distributors, hosts or private vendors the power to decide legal ownership, authorship, infringement, licensing validity, liability or enforcement.

An AURA manifest is not a legal judgment. It is not a takedown order. It is not a permission grant. It is not a waiver of rights.

Systems may use AURA artefacts as evidence or provenance signals, but any legal qualification remains a matter for law, audit, competent authorities, regulators, courts or contractual review.

## No Mandatory Technical Gate

AURA must not be used to argue that creators only have rights if they used AURA.

AURA must not become a mandatory technical gate for access to legal protection, distribution, remuneration, collective management, platform visibility or public funding.

AURA must remain useful even when a creator, institution or rightsholder chooses not to use it.

## What AURA Provides

AURA provides verifiable technical elements, such as:

- origin declaration data
- asset hashes
- issuer identifiers
- timestamps
- manifest signatures
- rights-reservation signals
- TDM opt-out signals
- public-key verification material

These elements may support documentation, audit, provenance, transparency and evidentiary workflows. They do not replace legal analysis.

## What AURA Does Not Provide

AURA does not provide:

- proof of legal ownership by itself
- proof of originality by itself
- automated infringement detection
- content recognition
- similarity analysis
- fingerprinting
- watermarking
- DRM
- monitoring
- platform enforcement
- automated sanctions
- automated legal decisions

## Governance Position

AURA must remain open, neutral, implementation-independent, privacy-preserving and resistant to capture by any single platform, vendor, model provider, repository host, institution or authority.

Any implementation, fork, platform integration or commercial service that claims alignment with AURA should preserve this position clearly and should not present AURA as a mechanism for removing rights, bypassing rightsholders or replacing legal institutions.
